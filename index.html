About

I like asking why people choose one brand over another, why one post gets saved and another gets scrolled past, and what a brand is actually saying when it isn't saying anything directly.

That curiosity is what pulled me towards marketing, communication and consumer behaviour. I learn best by doing: researching a brand, planning content, reaching out to creators, and watching what works. I also edit videos, which keeps the creative side of me busy.

Experiments open questions, not finished answers

  • Should captions follow the words or the voice?

    While helping with caption-synced typography on yapping-style Reels, I learned that hierarchy should follow vocal stress, not semantic importance.

  • Is a reactive brand a brand?

    At Airluxe I noticed the identity reacted to others instead of being original, the packaging was inconsistent, and the curly hair community was underserved.

  • Can MUNs be found in one place?

    I've been sketching a national MUN discovery platform for India. The hard parts are the cold start problem and giving organisers a reason to list.

  • What would a student's growth look like on one page?

    Early thinking on an ed-tech platform that follows a student's progression, not just their marks.
